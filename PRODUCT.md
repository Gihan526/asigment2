# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Students on campus who find an item or are looking for one they lost. They use the mobile app to report, search, and arrange collection while moving between campus locations.

## Product Purpose

Foundly helps students post found items with a photo and location, browse reports, and contact the finder so items can be returned to their owners.

## Brand Personality

Clear and practical. The interface should make the next action easy to find and use plain, direct language.

## Anti-references

Avoid duplicate calls to action, cluttered empty states, and wording that points to controls that are not there.

## Design Principles

- Show one clear primary action for each screen state.
- Keep reporting and searching quick on a phone.
- Use concise labels and examples that fit small screens.
- Preserve familiar mobile controls and feedback.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

No additional requirements were specified. Use readable text, sufficient contrast, clear labels, and comfortable tap targets; allow text to wrap or scroll when needed.
